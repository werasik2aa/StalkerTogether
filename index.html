Supported modpacks

GAMMA

The modpack significantly alters the classic gameplay to provide balanced survival, item gathering, cooking, crafting, and repair with long-term progression and smooth gameplay. Many add-ons were developed exclusively for this project.

EFP

EFP focuses primarily on tactical weapons and firefights. Who want an EFT-like experience. The developers aimed to make the modpack a close copy of EFT, but over time, it has evolved into its own unique thing. It also features some custom weapons.

CUSTOM

This modpack focuses more on visuals rather than gameplay—essentially a traditional Anomaly experience with enhanced graphics. It mostly consists of publicly available addons, though some modifications were created exclusively for this project.
