Our story

Anomaly Together 2.0 and Trilogy games

After the release of our version 2.0 in a separate https://discord.gg/mp-together, while continuing to develop a cooperative mode based on the Anomaly 1.5.3 mod, we were surprised to discover that our network code can be integrated into any FORC of the X-Ray engine, which means that the story of the first three parts can be run and completed if we adapt them to our code.

2025

New project - Anomaly Together

Despite the general skepticism and the precarious position of the project, as well as the deadlock in the development based on the engine X-RAY OMP 1.0. A developer came to us and offered to help us develop multiplayer inside the Anomaly 1.5.2 mod. So we basically started development from 0, from the idea to the first working version was exactly one month of development. Now, things have gotten good. With the past is over and we are the first who will give you the opportunity to play your favorite Anomaly, as well as builds on the network.

2024

Old developers

A black streak began in the life of the project and in my life. The old developers betrayed me, lying to everyone about my allegedly bad intentions, although I tried to do good and demanded mutual justice. During this period, not much was done, but the idea to make Anomaly on the custom network code appeared.

2023-2024

First release

After half a year of work, we released our first release, which turned out to be so stable and stress-resistant that we decided not to stop and decided to move on. We adapted Gunslinger weapon pack, added a lot of things, including redesigning maps for RP component.

15.05.2022-2023

X-RAY OMP

The project was created to make a platform for RP projects on the game Stalker based on the open source engine X-RAY OMP 1.0. The release was planned for foreign and Russian-speaking audience.
